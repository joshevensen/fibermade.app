## Spelling in site copy (marketing and legal pages)
- American English (colors, anonymized), even when the source wording uses British spelling — #11 — existing pages already use American spelling ("colors", "colorways")

## Wording of anonymization claims in legal copy
- State what the data contains ("contains no identifiers"), never that it "cannot be linked back to you" — #11 — re-identification from raw database access or backups remains possible, so the stronger claim would over-promise
